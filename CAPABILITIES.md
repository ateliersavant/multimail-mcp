## Known issues

- **Draft ids with a colon (fixed 2026-11-04).** Gmail gives some drafts an id of the form `s:` followed by digits, alongside the usual `r-` form. The server used to accept only letters, digits, `-` and `_` in ids, so `list_drafts` failed for a mailbox holding such a draft, and that draft could not be read, sent or deleted through multimail. Draft ids may now carry one colon; thread and message ids are checked as strictly as before, and every id is percent-encoded into a single path segment. Tested through every draft tool against a fake Gmail, and verified live on the mailbox where it failed.

## Does not

Direct send (only approved drafts are sent) · forward · cross-tenant operations · attachment upload or download · permanent deletion (`delete_draft` removes a draft; Trash is emptied by Gmail after 30 days) · spam marking · label creation · HTML bodies · settings or filter changes · non-Google mail · multiple principals.
